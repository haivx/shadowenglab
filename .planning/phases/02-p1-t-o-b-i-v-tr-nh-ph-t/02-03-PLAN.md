## Phase Goal

ROADMAP goal (verbatim): "Liam tạo bài từ link YouTube và tập trọn một video theo từng câu, đến bước ghi âm (chưa chấm điểm)." (not in user-story form; no story invented — see 02-01).

<objective>
Backend tracer of the phase: a lesson created over HTTP is processed in the background by an in-process job runner — downloads in parallel, model steps one job at a time in FIFO order — with retry and restart recovery. The API boundary is widened safely for the write endpoints that the rest of the phase adds, and every P1 router is mounted (empty routers are filled by 02-06, 02-07, 02-10, 02-11, 02-15, 02-16).

Purpose: LES-03 ("tạo 2 bài liên tiếp không chặn nhau"), LES-07, D-01, D-03; it proves the job architecture end to end before the rest of the backend expands it.
Output: `jobs.py`, `store.py`, `errors.py`, split `pipeline.py`, POST /lessons + retry, lifespan, middleware, seven router shells.
</objective>
