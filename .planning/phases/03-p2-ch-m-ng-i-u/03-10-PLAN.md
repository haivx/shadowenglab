`AttemptStrip` lists the sentence's scored attempts (`Lượt k · score`, best marked `tốt nhất`), clicking a chip shows that attempt's saved `ScoreResult`, `R` adds a new attempt without losing the best (FBK-06, D-11), and the listen buttons/shortcuts apply to the viewed attempt (M/B only when its blob is still in the session — FBK-04, D-11).

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 5 "Ghi lại (R) giữ lượt chấm tốt nhất".
Output: `lib/attemptChips.ts` (+ test), list/detail client + queries, updated `AttemptStrip`, `PracticeRoom`, `ResultActionBar`, `useShortcuts`.
</objective>
