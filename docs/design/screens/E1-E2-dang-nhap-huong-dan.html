E2 · Before you start

Rate how close the learner sounds to this speaker

Each clip has an original sentence from a video and a learner imitating it. Don’t judge whether the learner is “correct” English. Judge whether they sound like the original: if the speaker swallows a sound or says “gonna”, the learner should too.

The scale, with examples

5Could pass for the original speaker’s delivery.
3Clearly the same sentence, a few noticeable differences.
1Sounds very different: rhythm, melody or many sounds are off.

Flag words that sound different

  • Sound · a vowel or consonant differs (“and” said fully vs. “’n”)
  • Stress · a different word or syllable is emphasized
  • Pitch · the voice goes up where the original goes down, or stays flat
  • Pause · a break the original doesn’t have, or a missing one
  • Speed · this word is noticeably slower or faster
